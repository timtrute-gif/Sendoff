Got Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?
We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks out, especially for peak season (May–September). Popular experiences like yacht charters and VIP tables book up fast. That said, we've pulled together incredible weekends with as little as 2 weeks notice.
What's the typical group size you work with?
We handle groups from 6 to 50+. Our most common size is 10–16 guys, but we've organized everything from intimate 6-person trips to massive 40-person weekends with multiple party buses and venue buyouts.
Do you handle all the bookings, or do we?
We handle everything — venue reservations, transportation, accommodations, activity bookings, and coordination. You approve the itinerary and show up. That's it.
How does pricing work?
Every trip is custom-quoted based on your group size, dates, and selected experiences. There's no planning fee — we earn a small commission from our partner venues and services, so your quote is fully transparent. Per-person costs typically range from $300 to $1,500+ depending on the package tier.
Can we mix and match from different packages?
Absolutely. Our packages are starting points, not rigid templates. Want a yacht charter from the Yacht Weekend plus the golf round from the Golf & Brewery package? Done. Use our itinerary builder or just tell us what you're thinking.
What if plans need to change last minute?
Life happens. We work directly with all our partners to handle modifications as smoothly as possible. Each booking has its own cancellation window, which we'll make clear upfront. We've never left a group stranded.
